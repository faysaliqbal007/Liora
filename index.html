PRIVACY

Your saved records stay local.

Liora stores belongings and history in this browser. This prototype has no account, tracking, or public sharing.

Text messages and relevant item records are sent to AssemblyAI for AI responses. During voice mode, microphone audio is also sent to AssemblyAI for the live conversation. Avoid passwords, payment details, and sensitive personal information.
Using Liora

How Liora Works

Everything you need to get the most out of your voice-powered memory assistant.

mic

Telling Liora something

Speak or type naturally. Liora understands context — no special commands required. Just describe what happened.

Storing something

"My passport is in the bedroom drawer."

Packing something

"I packed my charger in my blue backpack."

Lending something

"I lent my camera to Sarah."

Borrowing something

"I borrowed Tom's umbrella."

search

Asking Liora questions

Ask in plain language. Liora searches your saved records and answers instantly.

chat

"Where did I keep my headphones?" → Liora tells you the location.

chat

"What have I lent out?" → Liora lists all your active loans.

chat

"What did I pack?" → Liora shows everything currently packed.

check_circle

Review before saving

Liora never saves a record without showing it to you first. After understanding what you said, it drafts a summary and asks you to confirm — nothing changes until you tap Confirm & save.

verified

You are always in control. Liora records nothing without your explicit confirmation.

record_voice_over

Voice Mode

Tap the blue microphone button at the bottom-right to start a live voice session. Liora listens, responds out loud, and can record items hands-free. Press End Session when you are done.

info

Voice mode requires microphone access and an active internet connection. Audio is processed by AssemblyAI to power the live conversation.

lightbulb

Tips for the best experience

star

Be specific about locations — "top shelf in the kitchen cabinet" is better than "kitchen".

star

Include the person's name when lending or borrowing — "I lent my drill to Ahmed" helps you search by name later.

star

Use the History page to review every confirmed move and handoff over time.

star

Avoid storing sensitive information like passwords or payment details.

verified_user

Private by design

No account required. All records are stored in your browser only. Nothing is uploaded or shared — except what you send to Liora's AI for processing, which is not retained.
